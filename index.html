AI ticket triage

What

Every ticket arrives classified, diagnosed, and contextualized before an IT admin opens it.

How

  • IT admins stop preparing tickets by hand
  • Live in hours: cloud to cloud, nothing to install
  • Triage rules written in plain English

Example

A “Can’t connect to VPN” ticket lands in HaloPSA. Automate sets the type and priority, adds the laptop’s network status from the RMM, and attaches the VPN article.

Context is written back to the ticket

  1. Triage note Adds a summary, sentiment, urgency, and entities.
  2. Type Sets type, subtype, and item from your definitions.
  3. Priority Sets priority from your definitions.
  4. DiagnosticsRMMMDMIdP Adds diagnostics using external integrations.

AI ticket resolution

What

Automate triages and resolves tickets, so IT admins only handle what needs a person.

How

  • Works in the ITSM you already have
  • Resolves tickets with built-in and custom skills
  • Routes what it can’t fix, with full context

Example

A “Locked out of my account” ticket lands in ConnectWise. Automate unlocks the account in Okta, notes what it did, and closes the ticket.

IT workflow automation

What

Automate runs onboarding, offboarding, and routine IT work when your systems trigger it.

How

  • No ticket needed: HR events, schedules, and webhooks start it
  • Works across your identity and device tools
  • Built-in and custom skills

Example

A new hire is added in the HR system. Automate creates their Entra ID account and adds their department’s groups before day one.
